Section: Fixed

**Keyboard focus.** The focus ring is 3px in the Secondary colour, 2px outside the control, or 3px inside the edge where the control is clipped. Cards, buttons, icon buttons, FABs, pickers, lists, the navigation rail, text fields, dialogs, sliders and search use that ring. A text field's own underline or outline, and a snackbar's inverse-surface ring, are unchanged.
