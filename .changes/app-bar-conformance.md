Section: Fixed

**App bars: the scrolled bar's elevation, slot colours, compressed rows, action colour, hide-on-scroll, toolbar spacing and navigation labels.** A scrolled top app bar takes the Level2 elevation its token names instead of Level1; its leading icon is OnSurface and its trailing icon OnSurfaceVariant, and a slotted bare button takes its slot's colour instead of the page's; a compressed medium or large bar keeps its navigation and actions beside the title at 64dp instead of hiding them; a title with no leading icon is inset 16dp, not 40dp. A bottom app bar's actions take OnSurfaceVariant and hide-on-scroll accumulates distance from the last turn of direction and marks the bar inert while it is off-screen, so a slow scroll now hides it and a hidden bar takes no focus. A docked toolbar keeps 4dp between crowded items, and a horizontal navigation bar's active label keeps Secondary, as the vertical one does. No API changes and no defaults that callers rely on.
