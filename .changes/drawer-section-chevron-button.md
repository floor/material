Section: Changed

**Navigation drawer.** A section header is now a label, and its chevron is a round icon button as tall as the row, ending on the active indicator's end, with its own state layer, focus ring, ripple, a touch target at least 48 dp wide and its own tab stop. Only the chevron opens and closes the section: the header takes no hover layer or tab stop. A collapsed section that holds the active destination carries the active indicator on its header. A section with `selectable: true` or an `href` (the `selectable` or `href` attribute on a parent item) is a destination of its own, with its chevron beside it. Sections open and close with the menu's scale reveal, retuned with `--mtrl-drawer-section-motion-duration`, `--mtrl-drawer-section-motion-easing` and `--mtrl-drawer-section-reveal-scale`, and rows under a header without an icon line up with the top-level rows.
