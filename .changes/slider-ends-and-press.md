Section: Fixed

**Slider ends and press.** The handle keeps the same inset from the track's ends whether the slider is continuous or has ticks, so at the minimum and the maximum it sits on the first and the last tick at every size, on both axes and in both directions, and a continuous slider's handle no longer reaches past the track's rounded end. Turning ticks on after the slider is made no longer leaves the handle at the position it had without them. A press on the handle, the active track or the inactive track shows the pressed 2 dp handle until the release, and a cancelled touch releases it too.
