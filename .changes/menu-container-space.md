**A menu inside a scrolling container.** With an explicit `container` the menu's side was chosen from the window alone, and its position was written without the container's own scroll: at 800×600 with a 160 px menu, a menu that the viewport had room for below opened where the container cut it (125 px clipped), a menu flipped above sat above the container's top (48 px clipped), and at scrollTop 120 the menu landed a scroll's worth too high, over its opener. Opening also focused the menu, and that native focus scroll the container — 120 to 40, 0 to 259 in the measured setups — moved the menu with it, so it ended up on its opener (gap −117 px). The side and the height cap now come from the part of the container's scrollport that is also in the window, the menu is written in that scrollport's local space in one conversion (its border, zoom and own scroll taken out and back), and focus inside a container menu never scrolls its ancestors: the menu's own list scrolls instead, so a keyboard End still reveals the last item in every layer — the body, the top layer and a container alike. Menus in the body and the top layer, which the window alone limits, are unchanged.
