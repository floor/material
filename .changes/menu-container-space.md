**A menu inside a scrolling container.** With an explicit `container` the menu's side was chosen from the window alone, and its position was written without the container's own scroll: at 800×600 with a 160 px menu, a menu that the viewport had room for below opened where the container cut it (125 px clipped), a menu flipped above sat above the container's top (48 px clipped), and at scrollTop 120 the menu landed a scroll's worth too high, over its opener. Opening also focused the menu, and that native focus scroll the container — 120 to 40, 0 to 259 in the measured setups — moved the menu with it, so it ended up on its opener (gap −117 px). The side and the height cap now come from the part of the container's scrollport that is also in the window, positions are written in the container's content space (its scroll added back), and focus inside the menu no longer scrolls its ancestors: the menu's own list scrolls instead when it must reveal an item. Menus in the body and the top layer, which the window alone limits, are unchanged.
