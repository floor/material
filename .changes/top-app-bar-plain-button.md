Section: Fixed

**Top app bar: a bare button in the leading or trailing slot shows a pressed state and keeps a 48px target.** A plain button in either slot had no pressed layer and no 48px hit target. It now draws an on-surface layer at the pressed opacity while held, and a 48px target centred on the button, at desktop and small-screen widths, for the factory and for `<m-top-app-bar>`. A 16px inline margin keeps a 16px leading button's target off the next bare button. An author class on the button, such as `nav-button`, still gets the layer. The rules ship in the top app bar stylesheet. The element shadow sheet reaches a slotted button with `::slotted()`, and the shared document base does not carry them. The resting box, padding and background stay as they are. Icon buttons and other library buttons keep their own layers.
