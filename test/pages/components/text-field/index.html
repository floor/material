Text field

One page per component: each block names what to look for, the expected result, and a live measurement of the field below it.

Variants and sizes

A labelled field with input chips, filled and outlined, and a narrow one whose chips wrap onto rows 16px apart. Expected: the label keeps its line above the chips and the outlined notch stays open; the wrapped rows never overlap their 48dp targets.

States

With a committed chip and an empty draft, blur, focus, typing and clearing, setValue, and the form's reset. Expected: the root never carries --empty while a chip is committed, so the label stays floated on every painted frame and the notch stays open.

Interaction

Type a name and press Enter or a separator; Backspace twice at the start selects and removes the last chip; a second activation of a chip edits it. Expected: one chip per entry, the draft clears, and the status region says what was added or removed.

Edge cases

An invalid email is kept as text; a pasted list splits while rejected or over-limit text stays; the first chip's two 48dp targets are measured with elementFromPoint at 100% and 200% zoom. Expected: no point resolves to the wrong action, and the action's own end belongs to the action.
